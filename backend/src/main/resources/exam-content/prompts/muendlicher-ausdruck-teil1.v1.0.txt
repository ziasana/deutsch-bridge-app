WHAT A TEIL 1 EXERCISE IS

In Teil 1 the learner and a partner get to know each other. The learner introduces themselves, asks the partner questions and answers the partner's questions. It is a real conversation, NOT a memorised monologue.

Every exercise contains the seven CORE topics, each with its exact id:
{{CORE_TOPIC_IDS}}
(name = Name; herkunft = Woher sie oder er kommt; wohnen = Wie sie oder er wohnt (Wohnung, Haus, Garten ...); familie = Familie; deutsch_gelernt = Wo sie oder er Deutsch gelernt hat; beruf = Was sie oder er macht (Schule, Studium, Beruf ...); sprachen = Sprachen (welche, wie lange, warum))

An exercise may add the OPTIONAL topics: {{OPTIONAL_TOPIC_IDS}} (wochenende = Wie die Person das Wochenende verbringt; hobbys = Welche Hobbys sie hat). Include them in most exercises.

The questions, follow-up questions and useful phrases of these topics are fixed and live in the Lernbereich - do NOT generate them.
For every topic provide ONLY:
- "id": the exact topic id.
- "exampleAnswers": 1-5 realistic spoken example answers (1-3 sentences each, first person). They show how to answer in a relaxed, natural way and must not read like a CV. All answers of one exercise belong to ONE consistent example persona.
In addition give "exampleProfile": one short German line that describes that persona (name, age, job, origin).
Vary the persona (name, country, job, family, languages) from exercise to exercise.

The exercise "topic" is a short German title, normally "Sich kennenlernen".
The "instructions" text is exactly: "{{INSTRUCTIONS}}"
