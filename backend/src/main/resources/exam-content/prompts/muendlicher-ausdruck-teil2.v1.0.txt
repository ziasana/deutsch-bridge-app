WHAT A TEIL 2 EXERCISE IS

In Teil 2 the learner receives a short opinion text from a person (a name, an age, an occupation and a statement about a topic, sometimes with a portrait photo). The learner then talks to a partner and must:
1. report the person's opinion (REPORT_OPINION),
2. express their own opinion (EXPRESS_OWN_OPINION),
3. describe their own experiences with the topic (DESCRIBE_EXPERIENCE),
4. react to the partner's opinion (REACT_TO_PARTNER).
These four communication goals and their useful phrases are the same in every exercise and live in the Lernbereich - do NOT generate them.

Every exercise provides:
- "topic": a short German title of the theme (e.g. "Gruppenreisen").
- "person": {"name": full name, "age": whole number, "occupation": job, "image": optional, "imageAlt": German description}. Use "image": "" and "imageAlt": "" when there is no portrait. If you give an image it must be an uploaded file path starting with /uploads/ or an https:// URL; imageAlt is then required.
- "opinionText": a coherent first-person opinion of about 60-120 words in {{LEVEL}} German. It gives the person's view with two or three reasons or examples (ich finde ..., weil ...). It must be balanced enough that learners can agree or disagree.
- "preparationNotes": optional, 0-8 short German hints for the preparation time (e.g. "Überlegen Sie, wann Sie zuletzt verreist sind.").
- "exampleResponse": optional, one coherent spoken example (about 120-200 words) that covers all four goals in a natural order.
Use different topics, persons and opinions in every exercise (travel, work, housing, learning, media, food, sport, shopping, free time ...).

The "instructions" text is exactly: "{{INSTRUCTIONS}}"
