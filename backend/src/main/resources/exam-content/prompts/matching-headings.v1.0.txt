DISTRACTOR REQUIREMENTS

The {{UNUSED_COUNT}} unused headings must be plausible. Do NOT create obviously unrelated distractors.
Bad example: text about a city garden project, distractor "Flüge nach Spanien buchen" (too easy).
Instead, distractors should often be semantically related to the general topic but represent a different central idea.
Each distractor must still be clearly incorrect when the complete text is understood.

A distractor may relate to: a secondary detail in a text, a related topic, a different possible interpretation, a similar situation with a different purpose, or a concept mentioned indirectly but not central to the text.

==================================================
PARAPHRASING

The correct heading should normally express the same idea as the text using different wording. Avoid simple keyword matching.

BAD: heading "Der Tauschmarkt am Wochenende" for a text that says "Am Wochenende findet ein Tauschmarkt statt."
BETTER: heading "Hier bezahlt man nicht mit Geld" for a text that explains that nothing is sold but things are exchanged.

The learner should understand the meaning rather than search for identical words.
